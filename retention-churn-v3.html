Pilot report/Retention and churn
MapleStory: Idle RPG × Oracle

Retention & Churn

Where new players drop off, how they behave before they quit — each proven on real recordings.

Section 1

Where new players stop, and what holds them up

What this is

361 people who installed MapleStory RPG Idle for the first time were recorded playing it. Each session was followed quest by quest through the first hundred guide quests. This cap provides a shared comparison window across the same stretch of the game. It allows time spent, stuck points, and player actions to be compared person to person. It does not limit how far players actually played.

Nexon supplied retention status for these accounts after the recordings were read. 103 players later churned and 74 stayed active. Those 177 are the only players used in any comparison. The other 184 recorded players appear in the problems section.

Every number says what it is out of. Every claim links to the second of footage behind it. A recording ending is capture stopping, not a player leaving. This report never treats an ended recording as a departure.

Player drop-off concentrates in the first ten quests and again between quests 51 to 100 rather than at a single quest. Early tutorial steps run cleanly, but late breakthrough stages take up to 1680 median seconds of active combat. Churned and active players display identical combat stats, progression speed, and loss counts at every shared quest milestone.

  • Dwell time consists of active play rather than idle time. Quest 71, Quest 75, and Quest 91 each record 0 median idle seconds. Quest 95 records 81 median idle seconds. Players actively fight and retry stages instead of idling.
  • The slowest quests are all chapter breakthrough stages. Quests 67, 71, 75, 79, 83, 87, 91, and 95 all require clearing Ellinia stages. These stages pit players against high-health bosses with strict stage timers. Players encounter repeated timeout defeats on these bosses open clip.

Nexon could lower boss health in Ellinia breakthrough stages to reduce long combat failure loops.

  • Churned and active players respond similarly to defeat. 40 of 103 churned players and 37 of 74 active players walked away from content they failed to beat.
  • Players often divert to other tasks or retry without upgrading after a loss open clip.

Section 3

Problems for the development team

3.1

Problems worth fixing

A collection of technical errors and design friction interrupted sessions across 361 recorded players.

Technical interruptions

  • Account linking and login fail with authentication errors on 10 of 361 players, showing messages like "(21013)", "(-10006)", or "authentication error 2001" open clip.
  • The client freezes or forces a restart after a session expiration dialog on 10 of 361 players, displaying "工作階段已到期" or "세션이 만료되었습니다." open clip.
  • Rewarded video ads fail to load on 6 of 361 players, showing errors such as "無法載入廣告", "The ad has not loaded.", or "광고가 로드되지 않았습니다." open clip.
  • Network delay spinners halt combat or login progression on 4 of 361 players, showing "Waiting for server response" ("正在等待伺服器回應...") open clip.
  • Entering a nickname causes client crash error 6-5001 or shows "You have not completed the preceding guide quest" on 3 of 361 players open clip.
  • Battle verification fails and desynchronizes combat on 2 of 361 players, terminating the session with errors such as penaltyUid mismatch open clip.
  • Enhancing potions is blocked when recovery exceeds max stats on 2 of 361 players, displaying "회복량이 최대 HP 또는 MP를 초과하여 강화할 수 없습니다." open clip.
  • Launching the game fails on 1 of 361 players due to "Security verification failed. result=1" while stalled at 1852MB open clip.
  • The client hangs indefinitely on a dark blue geometric loading screen on 1 of 361 players open clip.
  • A reward claim popup freezes across gameplay for 99 seconds on 1 of 361 players open clip.
  • Automatic hunting stops completely due to a full equipment inventory on 1 of 361 players open clip.
  • Sending chat messages is repeatedly blocked by text filtering on 1 of 361 players, displaying "有無法使用的文字。" open clip.

Payment failures

  • Store purchases and restores fail on 10 of 361 players, showing errors like "Unavailable in store. (errorCode: 9 - 100302)", "errorCode: 10 - 100107", or "Vendor not initialized" open clip.
  • Tapping purchase buttons disconnects the session on 2 of 361 players, showing "세션이 만료되었습니다" and forcing a restart open clip.
  • Buying offline mail rewards is blocked on 1 of 361 players by an incorrect "You don't have enough currency" error open clip.

Design friction

  • Changing nicknames in settings is blocked by guide quests on 6 of 361 players, or completes Guide Quest 72 merely by opening the window open clip.
  • Sending direct whisper messages in chat is blocked behind a Level 40 requirement on 1 of 361 players open clip.
  • The Stage 2-2 boss encounter displays a Slime sprite but shows Mano's name and health bar on 1 of 361 players open clip.

Nexon could fix store initialization and suppress session expiration popups during purchases and downloads.
