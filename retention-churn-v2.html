Retention & Churn

Where new players drop off, how they behave before they quit — each proven on real recordings.

Section 1

Where new players drop off first

1.1

The funnel from launch to core action

The route into the core combat-and-upgrade loop includes substantial waiting before play and detours into account management. Qxz1WYfU7ojEdOadZSgS8@15 9NuGau0P8ym5mIxW36gOn@127

  • The measurement covers 361 player journeys, from first launch through the first hundred guide quests. Each quest carries the player’s actions, elapsed time and game responses. The hundredth-quest cap puts retained and churned players on the same stretch of the game rather than comparing unequal amounts of play.
  • Journey assessment was blind to retention outcome. Nexon’s retention labels were attached afterwards: 103 players labelled churned and 74 labelled active.
  • Install is never on camera. The visible entry sequence starts with the client, authentication, resource downloads and loading. hPWVm5l6zjt7Bz6SCPFaA@16
  • The core action is not simply watching auto-combat. It includes upgrading, summoning, equipping and returning to a failed fight. rRGysN9dKeRsZ9g8hs5pW@284

1.2

At which step new users drop off first

The opening dialogue and first combat quest are the earliest visible interruption points, but they do not establish the first churn step. 2Bg9X3ZxGKN85ooFmWP6v@178 gywQIgdDyOhtxckNCGA0E@140

  • Among the 103 players labelled churned, 18 have no identifiable quest endpoint and 25 end within quests 1–10.
  • Quest 1, “Defeat enemies: 0/3”, has the largest numbered-step endpoint count among churned players: 13 of 103, against 4 of 74 active players.
  • Before combat, the opening dialogue can remain untouched until Sleep Mode starts. yrEKDDZ6hmXcmBx973gRK@385
  • At the first quest, a player opens the quit confirmation after the opening enemy encounter. gywQIgdDyOhtxckNCGA0E@140
  • An early logout can also be an account switch. A player logs out of the initial guest account to enter a named account on another server. 9NuGau0P8ym5mIxW36gOn@127

A recording ending means capture stopped, not that the player quit. Logout, a quit dialog or an app exit must be visible to identify leaving behaviour; a dialog alone does not prove the player confirmed it.

The first true new-user churn step requires timestamped quest and session events, Nexon’s retention-window definition, and account/server linkage that separates fresh users from returning players and rerolls.

Access to later systems

Lower social-system use among churned players partly reflects lack of access, not rejection of the feature.

  • Friends is used by 33 of 103 churned players and 49 of 74 active players, but is never available during the recorded journey for 61 of 103 churned players and 19 of 74 active players.
  • Social intent exists outside the active cohort: a churned player types “LFG” in public chat, while an active player searches for and sends friend requests. jfD6uwmpyUg9KrAgi5Gs4@453 QNmy3y1oApCZTFzyA9bub@25
  • Compare behaviour after feature access before treating social participation as a retention lever.

Combat recovery

Upgrading can close the loss loop immediately, but changing systems alone does not prove recovery.

  • Blue Mushmom failure leads to a Welcome Epic Weapon purchase, equipping Death Staff and a successful retry. mwBiGKYbA9atmWcIGGNC2@714 mwBiGKYbA9atmWcIGGNC2@726 mwBiGKYbA9atmWcIGGNC2@781
  • Against Faust, another player completes 2nd Job Advancement, enhances stats, awakens weapons and summons again, but still fails. wkGogz62H43UusMXFqcHw@481 wkGogz62H43UusMXFqcHw@601 wkGogz62H43UusMXFqcHw@837
  • Growth Guide should preserve the failed target and recommend an affordable next upgrade, then offer a return to that target. Menu activity alone is not the success criterion.

Unlocks and restrictions

An unlock announcement can leave the player farming indefinitely instead of using the new system.

  • “Go to Job Advancement” remains unused while another player continues auto-farming and watching world chat. vjRqTChOn3VrwqwV2Q5vE@226 vjRqTChOn3VrwqwV2Q5vE@827

What we found that nobody asked for

Monetization paths that fail

Purchase-ready players encounter store failures, including players who have already paid.

  • Store availability errors block further purchases. A previous Welcome Epic Weapon buyer repeatedly encounters “Unavailable in store. (errorCode: 9 - 100302)” on T1 Weapon Hot Deal. Another previous payer encounters the same error while attempting a Blue Diamonds purchase. D9I5QGdwrOlIIWs5DNhRo@1 qKKcXZrB28pTIbE7bqsHV@1
  • A purchase attempt can also break the session. Tapping the hot-deal purchase button is followed by “세션이 만료되었습니다”, a frozen client and a manual force quit. MGNeHQnmPrJROMAzyoJ8N@46
  • Vendor initialization interrupts more than one offer. T1 Weapon Hot Deal and 2nd Job Advancement Hot Deal produce errorCode: 9 - 100204 and repeated purchase attempts. FDThMlMtApSR1VwONfu20@2
  • Payment errors and delivery can disagree. ErrorCode: 10 - 100107 appears during purchases even though packages arrive in the mailbox shortly afterwards. Reconcile payment, delivery and error messaging before asking the player to retry. bPSsblTivny5Y9UGcQcnz@4
  • Rewarded-ad loading failures block both monetization and progression rewards. “無法載入廣告” prevents ad loading; “광고가 로드되지 않았습니다.” stops a skill-mastery acceleration attempt. AAmg7qumIamYRBpKhYnNX@128 MpUFd9ys5SxMKRfkkWXDW@711

Defects that break a player’s session

Authentication, loading and progression-state failures can prevent play even when the player keeps trying.

  • Repeated loading freezes prevent entry. The same player encounters a dark-blue geometric loading freeze across launches and returns the next day to try again. AXd3ecu9MjFfb2KzzyJNm@157 YLttdpTMarTszggHOZwyA@0
  • Account linking fails for a paying guest. A player buys Top Weapon Hot Sale, then encounters Nexon ID errors (21013), (-10006) and (1114), with captcha and recovery loops leaving linking unsuccessful. uHPbWwWho53ksfSopmxM3@0 utJ2JjsH2oLdwBg7W8KkF@215
  • Battle verification can terminate a session. A penaltyUid mismatch returns the player to the title screen during combat. wUZfFtFJoN1PXJw76Nb4F@280
  • Quest 40 can require an unstated prerequisite. HP Potion enhancement is rejected because recovery would exceed maximum HP; the player must increase maximum HP through stats and equipment before proceeding. Surface that prerequisite at the blocked action. dIjyXRNIn3d14xxN88VG3@106
  • Quest 72 can complete without a successful nickname change. Opening the input window registers completion even though submitted names are rejected. Completion should follow a successful save. 98TJVlWCr6omuvZRFW8xc@256
  • Inventory capacity can stop Auto Hunt. The player must use Dismantle All to recover. Warn and route to storage management before unattended hunting stops. tLrMgGjii58Bb3CYjf9K3@26

Behaviour worth knowing

Players bring class, identity and social goals into the idle loop, and some will restart an account to pursue them.

  • Account deletion is not always departure from the game. A player deletes a Striker account and starts again as Night Walker. A separate player switches from a fresh tutorial character to an established Bishop. Q3AQ2oGjWGx0gSt08udU3@180 OKrDRSJjBnPNLLIw1pMjd@54
  • Nickname entry can become a substantial task. One player spends about ten minutes trying unavailable names before securing “Midwhite”. Offer available alternatives and explicit validation rules. gHKmVeFQynJu4ciCh3elE@65
  • Friends can determine server choice. A player asks whether they can switch servers to join a friend, then encounters a level restriction when trying to whisper another player. 0ad9gc2SozYhwLB3ApXdR@86 0ad9gc2SozYhwLB3ApXdR@127
  • Cosmetic browsing can be the main activity, not a brief detour. One player spends over an hour across recorded sessions searching and previewing Costume items without purchasing or pushing quests. 9U0YSnoVj5Srkwpk8ptod@18
  • Higher rarity is not always an immediate upgrade. Equipping an unlevelled Unique weapon drops displayed Combat Power; the player restores the old weapon and upgrades the new one. Equipment recommendations should account for current level, not rarity alone. bx855hSGgC0xAEPkLIPBI@75
  • Early spending does not guarantee retention. A player labelled churned buys Ad Removal, membership and multiple summon passes during early play. Paid conversion and retention need separate success measures. ResHWL3mkbGJj8oA0SlM7@0
2.4

High-pLTV vs low-pLTV behavior

Answer: Using Nexon's own pLTV score: the HIGH tier (15.4% of players) generates 93.2% of purchases, and the sharpest separator is cadence — 2.3× more active days — not ticket size. On screen, the gap has a mechanism: HIGH players run a purchase-to-power loop (pay within 30 seconds, convert money into summons, clear the hardest content); LOW players are ad-dependent and wall-blocked, and their payment attempts die at the payment modal. Bonus finding: the unscored 69% hides dormant high-power veterans — accounts the pLTV model doesn't score.

The numbers (warehouse, scored population Jul 1 – Aug 15)

Tier Players Share of purchases Buyer conversion Active days Shop events/player
HIGH 84,563 (15.4%) 93.2% 60.0% 11.78 259
LOW 84,562 (15.4%) 4.2% 17.3% 5.05 78
ZERO (unscored) 380,454 (69.2%) 2.5% 1.1% — —

The inversion worth a campaign: ZERO out-ranks LOW on battle power (~9.9T vs ~288B; HIGH ~19.1T) and per-transaction spend (7,196 vs 3,100), and even character level (46.0 vs 42.4). The unscored bucket contains legacy high-power accounts gone dormant — the pLTV model's blind spot.

What this looks like on screen (30 players / 38 sessions)

  1. HIGH opens with the wallet: sessions that begin with a completed in-app purchase in the first 30 seconds, diamonds converted to bulk summons (333–3,000 pulls) within seconds, then straight into Zakum on Very Hard/Chaos difficulty.
  2. HIGH spends socially too: guild leadership, Master/Grandmaster arena with preset swaps, and 30k-diamond costume sets bought for display.
  3. LOW opens with the ad button: sessions start with reward claims and shop browsing; rewarded ads are the primary currency source; summons are gated by earned tickets (tens, not thousands).
  4. LOW is wall-blocked and modal-blocked: stuck at Ch 2–14 walls failing the same boss 2–6×; payment attempts cancelled or failing at the payment modal; cosmetic menus browsed but blocked by insufficient currency. The warehouse's 2.3×-days gap has this on-screen shape: the paid resource pipeline clears walls the free pipeline cannot.
  5. Paying doesn't immunize (cross-check from the churn side): whale and minnow wall anatomy is structurally identical — only the chapter differs (Ch 3-09 vs Ch 37-02); and one churned heavy spender bought a premium deal and cleared Very Hard Zakum in a final session before leaving.
